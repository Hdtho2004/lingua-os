# Lingua OS — Thọ / Learning Journey V3

## Scope
- 12-week roadmap, 24 original foundational lessons, quiz per lesson, flashcards and study sources.
- 72 minutes English + 48 minutes Chinese per day are study allocations, not automatically timed tasks.
- Exercises are knowledge checks; automatic completion does not prove speaking/listening proficiency.
- Only learner-authored material and short original examples are packaged. External sources are links, not redistributed copyrighted books, audio, or artwork.
- No commercial third-party API. IndexedDB local; JSON backup required between devices.

## Weekly rhythm (each of 12 weeks)
- Mon: initial learning and examples.
- Tue: listening via referenced sites + speaking.
- Wed: active recall; build original sentences.
- Thu: contrasting expressions and guided reading.
- Fri: independent listening and speaking + revisit errors.
- Sat: in-app knowledge quiz and additional productive task.
- Sun: delayed recall, journal reflection, review cards.

## Important notes
- First 12 weeks are tentative since no formal placement tests have been completed. English appears to need grammar and natural collocations; Mandarin assumed beginner.
- Optional linked content may be behind access conditions or change; copyright stays with owners.
- Prior v2 progress retained by merging only unseen lesson IDs.
- Restore will replace current browser data: export a backup first.
